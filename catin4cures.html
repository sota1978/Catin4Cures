Our Mission

Catin4Cure's main mission is to get patients and loved ones out of the hospital setting, giving them the opportunity to reconnect with nature, family, and themselves while on a dream outdoor adventure.
Quest to Cast Out Cancer

We also host various fundraising events such as raffles, silent auctions, community meals, and support groups to raise funds for our programs.

Healing through Nature

About Us

Catin4Cure's is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to providing cancer patients, survivors, and their families with life-changing outdoor adventures. We host the annual Katreash t. schweninger memoral cat fishing tournement, community meals, raffles, silent auctions, support groups, and more.

We support families in whatever way they have the most need, providing resources, adventures, and assistance tailored to their individual circumstances.

Our Flagship Event: Katreash T. Schweninger Memorial Annual Catfishing Tournament

Every summer, we host our signature event on the Red River of the North, bringing together fishing enthusiasts, families, and supporters for a day of fun and fundraising. All proceeds directly support our outdoor adventures for cancer patients and their loved ones, helping them cast out cancer through nature therapy.

Get involved by registering to participate, sponsoring a boat, or volunteering as a helper or fishing guide. Minnesota-based vendors are prioritized to keep it local.
